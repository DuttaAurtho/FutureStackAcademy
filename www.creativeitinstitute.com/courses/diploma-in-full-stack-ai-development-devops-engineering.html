Software development is evolving with Generative AI, intelligent applications, and cloud automation. Modern developers can combine full-stack engineering with AI-powered solutions and DevOps practices to build smarter products, automate workflows, and deploy reliable applications. Understanding these technologies together opens new possibilities for creating and maintaining modern software systems.

OVERVIEW

Course Overview

This Diploma in Full Stack Development, Generative AI & DevOps Engineering covers four core areas: frontend development with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React; backend development with Node.js, Express.js, and databases; Generative AI with LLMs, RAG, AI agents, and automation; and DevOps with Docker, CI/CD, AWS, Terraform, Kubernetes, and cloud deployment.
  • Frontend Development
  • Backend Development
  • Database Management
  • Generative AI and LLMs
  • RAG and AI Agent Development
  • AI Workflow Automation
  • DevOps and CI/CD
  • Cloud Engineering and DevSecOps
