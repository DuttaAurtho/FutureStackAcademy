About Us

Who We Are

Futurestackacademy is a Bangladesh based skill development and training institution focused on practical, career oriented, and industry aligned education.

We work at the intersection of digital skills, workforce development, AI readiness, and employability. Over the years, we have built a trusted platform for learners, corporate teams, academic institutions, and strategic partners.

Our Focus

What We Do

Building future ready skills through practical training, industry collaboration and innovative digital learning solutions.

  • AI & Future Skills

    AI literacy, prompt engineering, practical AI tools, and workforce readiness.

  • Workforce Development

    Train the trainer, employability, remote work readiness, and talent pipeline design.

  • Professional Digital Training

    Graphic design, digital marketing, software, UI/UX, cybersecurity, data, and more.

  • Industry Collaboration

    From youth and professionals to institutions for a better Asia.

  • Institutional Partnerships

    Programs for universities, schools, government projects, and development partners.
