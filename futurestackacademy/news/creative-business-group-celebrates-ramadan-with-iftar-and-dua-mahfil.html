Creative Business Group Celebrates Ramadan with Iftar and Dua Mahfil

To embrace the holy month together, Creative Business Group organized an iftar and a dua mahfil with its 12 sister concerns.

Dhaka, Bangladesh: Creative Business Group organized an iftar and a dua mahfil to embrace the holy month together at Star Kabab Restaurant on March 25, 2024. The company’s honorable Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Monir Hosen Sir delivered the welcoming address, wishing all the blessings of god and the joy of the upcoming Eid Festival.“ Working together towards excellence will strengthen our bond and help us bring success in the creative industry” he adds. More than 500 employees of the 12 sister concerns of Creative Business Group were present to be a part of the holy gathering. Chief Operating Officer Zia Uddin Mahmud and Chief Financial Officer Jamilur Rahman were also among the honorable guests. Before dua mahfil, the respected COO unveiled the Startup Excellence Award to all the employees to celebrate the outstanding achievement. The program ended with the hope that the institute would achieve significant accomplishments in the coming years.

 




Being the pioneer of the skill development program, Futurestackacademy, the subsidiary of Creative Business Group has been serving in the IT training industry for the past 15 years. Throughout the journey, this institute has created thousands of successful freelancers in Bangladesh who are currently working in different sectors. Futurestackacademy nurtures talent by sharing knowledge and helping students find the desired jobs to become financially solvent.
