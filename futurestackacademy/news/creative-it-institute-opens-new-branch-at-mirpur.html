Futurestackacademy Opens New Branch at Mirpur

July 6, 2024: Futurestackacademy celebrated its continuous journey towards learning with the opening of a new branch at Mirpur 10, Dhaka on July 1. The institute looks forward to providing all the facilities to the students to ensure a great learning experience.

The honorable Chairman of Creative Business Group, Monir Hosen and the Chief Operating Officer Ziauddin Mahmud inaugurated the program with a ribbon-cutting ceremony. The program started with the holy Quran tilawat and after that, the CEO provided an opening speech highlighting the vision of the institute and its progress as one of the best IT training institutes in the country.


“We aim to provide our services at the doorstep of people who aspire to learn and develop themselves. This starting marks another step towards making skilled manpower, now the students of the surrounding areas can commute easily and save time,” said the CEO. 



Among others, Shomi Kaiser, President of e-Commerce Association of Bangladesh, M Rashidul Hasan, Vice president of the Bangladesh Association of Software and Information Service, MD. Ali Noor, Vice-Chancellor of Bangladesh University of Business and Technology was present to share the moment with everyone. 


With the opening of this branch, Futurestackacademy now has a total of 3 branches in Dhaka that are fulfilling the growing demand of the students. The students living in Mirpur, Bijoy Sarani, Agargaon, Pallabi, Kazipara and surrounding areas will be able to continue their classes on the new campus. The five most demanding courses - Professional Digital Marketing, Professional Graphic Designing, Video Editing, Front End Development With React, and UI/UX Design are available for enrollment. 


The admission process is going on in full swing.
