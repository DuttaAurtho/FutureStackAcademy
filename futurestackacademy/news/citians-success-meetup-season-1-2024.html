CITians Success Meetup - Season 1 (2024)

Futurestackacademy organizes CITians Success Meetup - Season 1 (2024) to celebrate the achievements of the students who have made a successful remark in the IT industry. Many renowned faces of the industry participated in the daylong program to share their experience with the students.

Futurestackacademy arranged CITians Success Meetup - Season 1 at the main campus on 11th May 2024. The program was organized to encourage students who are successfully working in the IT industry. Many renowned faces of the industry were present in the daylong program and shared their experience to lead the learners in their upcoming journey. 


The program started with the Quran Tilawat, followed by the national anthem. The Chairman of Creative Business Group Mr. Monir Hosen gave his welcome speech to all the students and thanked them for participating in the program. He also expressed his gratitude to them for representing the institute on diverse platforms. Later, the Chief Operating Officer Mr. Zia Uddin Mahmud conducted a leadership session to guide them toward career success. There was an ice-breaking activity in the middle of the session. Afterwards, a crest-giving ceremony was arranged to celebrate the achievements of the students. 



Among the special guests, there was Mr. Ariful Hasan Opu, President of Bangladesh Innovation Forum, Md. Altamis Nabil, Head of Business and Engagement at BDapps, Barrister Hamidul Mishbah, President of Bangladesh Organization for Learning and Development, and KM Hasan Ripon, Executive Director and Principal in Charge of Daffodil Institute of Engineering and Technology. Famous YouTuber Tawhid Afridi and Tabib Mahmud were also present to accompany the students in the fun activities. 


Through the program, the students strengthened their bonds and learned valuable lessons to face the challenges in the evolving tech industry. They shared the stories of overcoming their problems with the assistance of Futurestackacademy. Doin Tech, Nextweb, Arouse Digital are some of the many successful platforms where former students are working in leading positions. Besides, this meetup provided refreshments to all, especially the indoor games were a great source of joy for most. Many of them showcased their talents in the cultural program. The participants said that the event made them enthusiastic and they expect to join more events like this to have some respite in their busy lives.
