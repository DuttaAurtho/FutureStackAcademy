CAREER PLACEMENT

Overview

Plenty of jobs are out there, but many skilled people still can't land the right one. Maybe they need career advice, more interview confidence, or a better CV. Our Career Placement Department (CPD) helps with all of it. We help you write a CV that passes company screening software, practice with mock interviews, build your communication skills, and meet industry experts. We also work with top companies to open doors for you. We support you from your first CV draft to your first day at work. Our goal isn't just to get you a job, but to help you build a career you feel confident about.

  • Dedicated career placement team
  • 3000+ partner companies
  • CV, portfolio & interview support
  • Support long after graduation
