OUR MILESTONES

Our Milestones

We contribute to the path of progress to turn the workforce into a tech-savvy generation. Since 2018, we have successfully trained more than 1,00,000 people, many of whom are successfully working in the global market.

ASOCIO Talent Development Award 2026

In recognition of its 18 years of continuous contribution to digital talent development, Futurestackacademy has received the ASOCIO Talent Development Award 2026—a proud international milestone for Bangladesh’s skills ecosystem.

Yusuf Chowdhury Award 2020

Inspired by the slogan 'We won't look for jobs, we'll create them', Futurestackacademy received the Yusuf Chowdhury Award 2020 for its role in developing entrepreneurs. The institute earned this recognition from among more than two hundred organisations.

JCI TOYP Award 2021

In recognition of his exceptional contribution to entrepreneurship development, founder Mr. Monir Hosen received JCI Bangladesh's 'The Ten Outstanding Young Persons of Bangladesh (TOYP) 2021' award.
