PRACTICAL LEARNING

Projects you'll actually build

Every project is portfolio-grade and presented with mockups, the way a client would receive it.

Facebook Page Creation & Optimization

Create and fully optimize a Facebook Business Page for a mock or real business, focusing on branding, keyword-rich content, and complete information architecture to maximize organic reach.

Facebook Ad Campaign Execution

Plan, build, and launch a complete end-to-end Facebook Ad campaign, targeting a specific funnel stage (Awareness, Engagement, or Lead Generation), and analyze its performance metrics.

Pixel & Conversion API Setup

Implement advanced tracking by installing the Facebook Pixel and configuring the Conversions API on a website to ensure accurate data collection for campaign optimization and attribution.

Messenger Chatbot Automation

Build an automated lead generation or customer service chatbot using ManyChat or Meta Business Suite to engage with page visitors and qualify leads without manual intervention.

Instagram Business Profile Creation & Optimization

Create and fully optimize an Instagram Business Profile for a mock or real brand, focusing on visual branding, keyword-rich bio, content strategy, and engagement features to maximize organic reach and audience growth.

Upwork Freelancer Profile & Proposal Setup

Create and fully optimize an Upwork freelancer profile, craft a winning proposal, and develop a strategic pricing model to attract high-value clients and secure projects in your chosen niche.

YouTube Channel Creation & Video SEO Optimization

Create and fully optimize a YouTube channel for a specific niche, develop a content strategy aligned with audience interests, and implement advanced video SEO techniques to maximize visibility, subscriber growth, and viewer retention.

LinkedIn Professional Branding & Content Strategy

Create and fully optimize a professional LinkedIn profile, develop a consistent content calendar focused on industry insights, and implement engagement strategies to build thought leadership and generate quality B2B leads.

Mailchimp Email Campaign & Automation

Create and execute a complete email marketing campaign using Mailchimp, including list building, segmentation, automated welcome sequences, and performance analysis to drive engagement and conversions.

Pinterest Business Account & Content Strategy

Create and optimize a Pinterest Business account, design and schedule keyword-rich Pins across multiple boards, and implement Rich Pins to drive website traffic and brand engagement.

TikTok Business Account & Viral Content Strategy

Create and optimize a TikTok Business account, produce engaging and trend-aligned video content, and implement growth strategies to increase followers, engagement, and brand visibility.

X (Twitter) Business Profile & Engagement Strategy

X (Twitter) Marketing make a project create and optimize an X (Twitter) Business profile, develop a content calendar with engaging tweets and threads, and implement interaction strategies to grow followers and enhance brand presence.

Social Media Budget Plan & Client Pitch Presentation

Develop a comprehensive social media budget plan for a mock business, and create a compelling client pitch presentation that outlines strategy, projected ROI, and campaign deliverables.
