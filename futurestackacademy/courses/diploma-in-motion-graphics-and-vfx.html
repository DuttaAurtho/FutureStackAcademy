COURSE OVERVIEW

This Diploma in Motion Graphics & Visual FX takes you from animation principles to logo animation, kinetic typography, 2D/3D motion, VFX and cinematic promos in After Effects, Premiere Pro, Animate CC and Blender. With 69+ industrial projects, mentor support and freelancing guidance, you build a portfolio for client-based work.
